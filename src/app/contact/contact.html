Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new task?

Click the "Enter Task" button in the To Do column and add your task details.

Can I move tasks between columns?

Yes. Simply drag and drop the task card into another column.

Can I add new workflow columns?

Yes. Use the "Add Column" feature at the bottom of the board.

Are my tasks saved automatically?

Your tasks are stored locally in your browser using local storage.
